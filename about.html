Our Story

About SheRises

A movement born from faith, built on purpose, and committed to seeing every individual rise into their God-given potential.

Leadership Development • Empowerment • Capacity Building

Our Story

SheRises Collective was founded with a simple yet profound conviction: that every person carries within them a divine purpose waiting to be unlocked. What began as a small gathering of women seeking deeper connection with God and each other has grown into a movement touching lives across communities.

Rooted in the belief that leadership is a calling, not merely a position, we create spaces where faith and practical development intersect. Our programmes are designed to meet people exactly where they are — whether at the beginning of their journey or ready to step into new levels of influence.

"We don't just teach leadership. We cultivate leaders who lead from a place of wholeness, authenticity, and spiritual depth."

Based in Pinetown, KwaZulu-Natal, SheRises Collective serves communities throughout the greater Durban area and beyond, partnering with churches, schools, and organisations to bring empowerment where it's needed most.

🔭

Our Vision

To raise empowered, healed, and purpose-driven individuals who can lead effectively, tell their stories with confidence, and positively transform their communities.

🎯

Our Mission

To develop people through structured programmes, mentorship, creative expression, and spiritual formation that strengthen leadership capacity, emotional resilience, and personal growth.

What We Focus On

Five interconnected areas through which we develop leaders and transform communities.

🎓

Leadership & Capacity Building

Leadership development programmes, emerging leader training, team-building, and public speaking coaching.

✍️

Writing & Story Development

Writing workshops and retreats, life story and memoir development, editing support, and reflective journaling.

🧭

Mentorship & Coaching

Individual and group mentorship, personal development coaching, purpose discovery, and emotional healing sessions.

🌿

Retreats & Transformation

Healing and restoration retreats, spiritual growth weekends, and community reflection and wellness gatherings.

🤲

Community Empowerment

Women and youth development, family and children's engagement, and community leadership initiatives.

Our Core Values

These principles guide every decision, programme, and interaction at SheRises Collective.

⚡ Excellence

We do all things with care, integrity, and professionalism — good enough is never our standard.

💗 Compassion

We create safe, respectful, and healing-centred spaces where every person is met with dignity.

🤝 Collaboration

We build together and honour every contribution. No one is meant to walk this journey alone.

🙏 Spiritual Maturity & Integrity

We lead with character and accountability, seeking God's guidance in all we do.

🌱 Servant Leadership

We prioritise people and their growth, believing true leadership is a calling to serve.
