Meet the Visionary

The Founder

Prophetess, Author, Speaker & Leadership Developer

With a heart burning for God and a vision to see every individual rise into their divine purpose, the founder of SheRises Collective has dedicated her life to empowering others through faith, teaching, and authentic leadership.

Fikile Mnguni's journey into education and community development began overseas, where she taught in the United Kingdom before returning home to South Africa to complete her Master's degree in Education. She went on to found Ibanga Academy, a teacher-training institution where the majority of graduates have found employment in early childhood education.

She later worked at C.A.S.T. as Project Manager, leading a range of outreach initiatives and community development programmes. Today, she teaches isiZulu part-time at Ingane Yami orphanage school and trains the housemothers there in leadership skills, alongside her work as a GEDSI (Gender Equality, Disability and Social Inclusion) specialist — helping ensure projects cater to and accommodate a wide range of demographics.

"I believe that when one woman rises with God, she doesn't rise alone. She becomes a bridge for others to cross over into their destiny."

Fikile also offers private isiZulu tuition and adult conversational isiZulu classes, which she sees as an important step in connecting cultures and building a sense of unity — breaking down the language barrier within KwaZulu-Natal.

The Journey

A timeline of faith, obedience, and divine appointments.

2021
The vision for SheRises Collective was birthed in prayer — a calling to gather, equip, and send forth leaders who would transform their communities.
2022
First gathering held with a small group of women seeking deeper connection with God. The room was filled with tears, breakthrough, and the beginning of something beautiful.
2023
Expanded into leadership development programmes, workshops, and community outreach. Partnerships formed with local churches and organisations.
2024
First book published. Speaking engagements multiplied. The message of rising began to echo beyond Pinetown into new cities and communities.
2025
Launched the Flourish Expo and Durban International Book Fair presence. SheRises Collective became a recognised name in empowerment circles.
2026
A season of expansion — new programmes, new partnerships, and a renewed commitment to seeing thousands rise into their God-given purpose.
