Our Leadership

Each member brings unique gifts, passion, and commitment to the SheRises vision.

Wesleigh Couch

Social Media & Marketing Intern

I am a creative driven by a passion for photography, visual storytelling, and purposeful design. I strive to create compelling work that captures authentic moments, conveys meaningful messages, and inspires audiences through both art and faith. At SheRises Collective, I serve as Social Media & Marketing Intern, planning and scheduling content, designing and coordinating promotional materials, and writing the newsletters, blogs, and media releases that carry our story. Through strategic communication and impactful creative content, I help advance the organisation's mission, strengthen connections with its community, and expand its reach and influence.

✉️

Yani Mnguni

Head of Operations & Community Coordinator

Yani keeps SheRises Collective running behind the scenes and connected out front. He trains new interns, oversees their work, and reviews their progress reports, while also attending and helping facilitate the organisation's functions and events. He manages sales, the company website, and the internal database, ensuring the systems that support the team stay dependable and up to date. His steady hand across operations and community makes him a vital bridge between SheRises and the people it serves.
