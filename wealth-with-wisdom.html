Facilitators

The programme is facilitated by experienced Christian leaders, entrepreneurs, financial practitioners and subject-matter specialists, combining biblical principles with practical financial and leadership knowledge.

What You'll Receive

  • Programme workbook
  • Module notes
  • Practical financial planning tools
  • Wealth-building exercises
  • Business and enterprise planning templates
  • Recommended resources

Who Should Attend

  • Pastors, church leaders, ministry leaders or recognised emerging leaders
  • Those able to commit to attending sessions
  • Willingness to participate actively in discussions and practical activities
  • Ability to complete required assignments and exercises
  • Commitment to developing a Personal Wealth-Building & Stewardship Plan

No formal financial qualification is required.

Graduation

The programme concludes with a Graduation Celebration on 17 July 2027, recognising participants who have completed the programme requirements.

Delivery Options

Choose the format that works best for you.

In Person

💻

Hybrid

Join the scheduled sessions online while engaging with the programme remotely.

Bursaries & Sponsorships: There are currently no bursaries or sponsorships available for this programme.
