SheRises Collective will be attending the Christmas Market at the Phansi Museum — come find our stall! We'd love to connect with you in person. Come along for a festive day out, browse the stalls, and support local vendors.

The market takes place at the Phansi Museum, 500 Esther Roberts Road, Glenwood, from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

Would you like to trade? Vendors are wanted for the festive Christmas Market. Stall cost is R150 (table supplied). For more information about the market itself, contact Nikki.
