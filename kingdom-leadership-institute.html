Certificate in Kingdom Leadership

Kingdom Leadership Institute

Foundations, Development and Practice of Kingdom Leadership

"Equipping Leaders. Strengthening Churches. Transforming Communities."

The Kingdom Leadership Institute exists to equip pastors, church leaders, Christian professionals and emerging leaders with biblical, strategic and practical leadership skills to lead healthy churches, develop effective leaders and transform communities for the Kingdom of God.

This leadership journey, offered over six or twelve months, combines biblical teaching, practical leadership development, mentoring and strategic ministry planning to prepare leaders for greater Kingdom impact.

About the Institute

Equipping Kingdom leaders to faithfully steward the leadership entrusted to them by Christ.

The Kingdom Leadership Institute is an initiative of SheRises Collective, established to equip, develop and mentor Kingdom leaders for faithful and effective service in the Church and society.

The Institute recognises that God calls people to leadership in different contexts. Some lead established churches, while others serve in emerging ministries, rural communities, missionary work, workplaces, schools, businesses and community organisations. Many have a clear calling and practical experience but need structured biblical teaching, leadership development and mentorship to strengthen their service.

Our programme provides a structured learning journey grounded in Scripture and focused on Christ-centred leadership, spiritual maturity, accountability, effective ministry, mentorship and practical community impact.

Programme Structure

Two levels of study — choose the one that fits your leadership journey.

Level 1: Foundations of Kingdom Leadership

This level establishes the biblical foundations of leadership. Participants explore Christ's example, the character of a Kingdom leader, the stewardship of calling and gifts, spiritual authority, accountability, healthy ministry structures and service.

Duration: 6 monthsFee: R658 per monthTotal: R3,948
Month 1

Biblical Foundations of Kingdom Leadership

Participants explore Christ's authority over His Church and the biblical meaning of leadership as service and stewardship.

  • Christ as Head of the Church
  • Jesus Christ as the Servant King
  • Leadership, calling and position
  • Leadership as stewardship rather than ownership
  • The difference between Kingdom leadership and worldly models of leadership

Key Scriptures: Colossians 1:15–20; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:1–17

Month 2

Biblical Models of Kingdom Leadership

This module examines the character and values that shape a faithful Kingdom leader.

  • Biblical examples of leadership
  • Character before competence
  • Humility, integrity and obedience
  • Spiritual maturity and the fruit of a leader's life
  • Servant-hearted leadership in practice

Key Scriptures: 1 Timothy 3:1–13; Titus 1:5–9; Philippians 2:1–11

Month 3

The Weight of Leadership — Calling, Anointing and Stewardship

Participants examine the sacred responsibility associated with leadership entrusted by God.

  • Understanding calling and spiritual gifts
  • The anointing and the responsibility to serve faithfully
  • Exercising spiritual authority under Christ
  • Stewarding God's people, gifts and opportunities
  • Remaining dependent on the Holy Spirit
  • Accountability before God

Key Scriptures: Romans 11:29; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2; 2 Corinthians 4:1–7; Hebrews 13:17

Month 4

Spiritual Authority, Accountability and Ethical Leadership

This module explores how authority should be exercised with integrity, humility and accountability.

  • Biblical principles of authority and submission
  • Leadership structures and accountability
  • Ethical conduct and transparency
  • Recognising and preventing the misuse of spiritual authority
  • Appropriate leadership boundaries and responsible decision-making

Key Scriptures: Mark 10:42–45; 1 Peter 5:1–4; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21

Month 5

Building Healthy Churches and Ministry Teams

Participants consider the structures, relationships and practices that support healthy churches and effective ministry.

  • Biblical governance and ministry responsibilities
  • The roles of pastors, elders, deacons and ministry teams
  • Delegation and teamwork
  • Conflict resolution
  • Financial stewardship and accountability
  • Developing healthy, collaborative leadership teams

Key Scriptures: Acts 6:1–7; Acts 14:21–23; 1 Corinthians 14:40

Month 6

Leadership in Action — Service, Mentorship and Community Impact

This module connects biblical leadership principles with practical service.

  • Discipleship and serving others
  • Identifying and developing emerging leaders
  • Mentorship and leadership multiplication
  • Understanding community needs
  • Collaboration across churches and organisations
  • Preparing a practical leadership action plan

Key Scriptures: Matthew 28:18–20; Ephesians 4:11–16; Jeremiah 29:7

Level 2: Developing Kingdom Leaders

This level builds on the foundations established in Level 1. Participants develop skills in vision and strategic planning, mentorship, leadership multiplication, community collaboration, financial stewardship, sustainable service and succession planning. Progression to Level 2 is subject to the Institute's programme requirements.

Duration: 12 monthsFee: R350 per monthTotal: R4,200 per annum
Month 1

Strategic Leadership and Vision Stewardship

Participants learn to translate vision into purposeful and responsible action.

  • Discerning and communicating vision
  • Aligning ministry activities with the vision
  • Strategic planning and setting priorities
  • Managing resources responsibly
  • Monitoring progress and evaluating outcomes

Key Scriptures: Habakkuk 2:2–3; Proverbs 16:3; Nehemiah 2:11–18

Month 2

Mentorship, Discipleship and Leadership Multiplication

This module focuses on equipping others and preparing leaders for the future.

  • Recognising gifts and potential in others
  • Building effective mentoring relationships
  • Discipleship and spiritual formation
  • Delegating responsibility and developing confidence
  • Preparing emerging leaders to carry responsibility faithfully

Key Scriptures: 2 Timothy 2:1–2; Ephesians 4:11–16; Numbers 11:16–17

Month 3

Leadership in Action — Church and Community Collaboration

Participants explore how churches and Kingdom leaders can contribute to stronger communities.

  • The role of the Church in community transformation
  • Building partnerships with businesses, NGOs and public institutions
  • Responding to family, youth and community needs
  • Encouraging collaborative initiatives across churches
  • Planning practical community development projects

Key Scriptures: Jeremiah 29:7; Matthew 5:13–16; Galatians 6:9–10

Month 4

Enterprise, Financial Stewardship and Economic Empowerment

This module considers the responsible management of resources and the contribution Kingdom leaders can make to economic development.

  • Biblical principles of stewardship
  • Financial integrity and responsible resource management
  • Entrepreneurship and sustainable enterprise
  • Skills development and economic empowerment
  • Supporting initiatives that create opportunities for young people and communities

Key Scriptures: Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 22:29; Luke 16:10–12

Month 5

Resilient Leadership, Healthy Boundaries and Sustainable Service

Participants consider how leaders can sustain faithful service while caring responsibly for themselves and others.

  • Recognising the pressures associated with leadership
  • Healthy boundaries and sustainable ministry practices
  • Rest, prayer and dependence on God
  • Responding constructively to conflict and difficult seasons
  • Understanding when specialist support or referral may be needed
  • Building supportive leadership relationships

Key Scriptures: Mark 6:30–32; Galatians 6:2–5; 1 Kings 19:1–18

Month 6

Legacy, Succession and the Faithful Steward

The final module brings together the learning journey and prepares participants to apply what they have learned.

  • Faithfulness in fulfilling God's purposes
  • Leadership legacy and succession planning
  • Developing others to carry responsibility
  • Reviewing personal growth and leadership practice
  • Presenting a practical leadership impact project
  • Preparing for continued mentorship and leadership development

Key Scriptures: 2 Timothy 4:6–8; 1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15

Months 7–12

Practical Leadership Development and Application

Applying biblical leadership principles in ministry and community settings • Implementing and reviewing a leadership action plan • Mentorship and peer learning • Strengthening governance and accountability practices • Developing and evaluating a community impact initiative • Preparing a final leadership development portfolio and presentation

How Learning Takes Place

Delivery

Online facilitated learning, one session on the first Saturday of each month.

Learning Approach

Biblical teaching, guided discussion, reflection, practical exercises and application to participants' leadership contexts.

Practical Application

Participants will work towards applying their learning in their churches, ministries, workplaces or communities.

What You Will Need

An internet-connected device, and provision for data. Further information about learning materials, participation requirements and any assessments will be provided during registration.

Completion & Recognition

Participants who meet the Institute's stated participation and completion requirements will be eligible for the relevant programme completion certificate and graduation arrangements.

The Kingdom Leadership Institute is being developed as a non-accredited leadership development programme. Its certificates recognise completion of the Institute's programme and are not presented as nationally accredited qualifications.

The Outcome

Our desire is to see Kingdom leaders grow in biblical understanding, character, accountability and practical leadership ability. We want participants to serve faithfully under the authority of Christ, strengthen the people entrusted to their care, develop emerging leaders and contribute meaningfully to the communities in which God has placed them.

The call is to lead under Christ's authority, serve with humility, steward faithfully and advance the purposes of God's Kingdom.
