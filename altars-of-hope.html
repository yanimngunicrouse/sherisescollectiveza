Our Purpose

Altars of Hope is a creative storytelling and reflection experience designed to help participants pause, remember, and celebrate God's faithfulness throughout their journey.

Through guided reflection, storytelling, creativity, prayer, and connection, participants will create a personal Altar of Hope — a visual reminder of God's grace in their lives and the hope that lies ahead.
