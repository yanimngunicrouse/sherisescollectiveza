CRUX — Conversations at the Heart of Christian Leadership — A SheRises Collective Programme

CRUX is a Christian leadership conversation programme of SheRises Collective that brings Christian leaders into meaningful conversations about the issues at the heart of Christian leadership.

Through conversations with leaders from different backgrounds, generations and areas of influence, CRUX explores calling, character, servant leadership, integrity, influence, authority, responsibility, relationships, succession, legacy and the realities of leading in faith and service.

CRUX provides a space for leaders to share their experiences, insights and lessons learned, while inviting audiences to reflect more deeply on what it means to lead as a Christian.

The programme is built around thoughtful questions, honest conversations and practical wisdom, with each conversation offering something leaders can carry into their own calling.

Programme Purpose

CRUX creates a space for meaningful conversations with Christian leaders about the realities of leadership. The programme explores calling, character, service, influence, responsibility, relationships, integrity, growth and legacy, while giving leaders an opportunity to share the wisdom they have gained through experience.

CRUX is intended to help leaders think, reflect, learn and grow.

Who CRUX Is For

CRUX is for:

Christian leaders currently serving in ministry
Emerging and aspiring Christian leaders
Church leaders
Ministry workers
Christian professionals and entrepreneurs
Young leaders
People interested in Christian leadership and leadership development

It should be accessible to someone who is leading a church, a ministry, a business, an organisation, a community, or simply beginning to understand.
