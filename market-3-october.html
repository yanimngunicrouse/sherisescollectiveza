SheRises Collective is hosting our very first market at a new location, and we're looking for vendors to be part of something great! Whether you sell books, crafts, food, clothing, or another product or service, this is a wonderful opportunity to connect with the community and grow your business alongside like-minded entrepreneurs. Come along for a fun day out, browse amazing stalls, and support local vendors.

The market takes place at Hillcrest Collegiate Private School, 35 Mill Road, Hillcrest, from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Vendor spaces are limited, so early enquiries are encouraged.
